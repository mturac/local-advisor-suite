# KB Entry — <ID>

> Skeleton for the knowledge-capture skill. One reusable question-and-answer
> per entry; fields match the kb-add / kb-search CLI so entries are
> interchangeable with the knowledge ledger.

- **ID**: <KBn>
- **Question**: <the question as a future reader will phrase it>
- **Answer**: <the answer, one paragraph, self-contained>
- **Source**: <where this answer comes from — file, decision, incident>
- **Tags**: <comma,separated,tags>
- **Captured**: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Expires / re-check**: <date or "never">

## Quality bar

- The answer must stand alone: no "see above", no pronouns without
  referents.
- The question is phrased the way someone would search for it, not the way
  it was first asked.
- If the answer may go stale (versions, prices, team structure), set a
  re-check date — the audit flags entries older than their expiry.
